Help & Instructions

Use this guide during setup, gameplay, review, scoring, and Teams leaderboard posting.

How to Play

  1. Enter your name.
  2. Select Easy, Medium, Hard, or Full Library.
  3. Choose Real Photo or AI Generated before time expires.
  4. Review clues after each answer.

Scoring

  • Correct answer: 1 point.
  • Incorrect or timeout: 0 points.
  • Final score and accuracy appear at the end.

Timer & Pause

  • Each round starts with 30 seconds.
  • Warning beeps play near the end when sound is on.
  • Pause for Review stops the timer for discussion.

Spotting AI Clues

  • Inspect hands, limbs, reflections, badges, labels, small text, and edges.
  • Look for repeated patterns, warped geometry, and blended objects.
  • Verify before you trust visual information.

Leaderboard

  • Scores are saved locally in the browser.
  • Export the leaderboard to CSV for recordkeeping.
  • Optional Teams posting uses an approved Power Automate Teams workflow URL.

Certificate

  • Download a PNG certificate.
  • Print or save the certificate as PDF.
  • The certificate includes name, score, date, and company branding.

Ethics reminder: If something looks authentic, that does not mean it is verified. Pause, inspect, and validate before relying on visual information.
